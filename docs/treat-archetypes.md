# Treat Archetypes — proposed reorganization (2026-08-02)

> **Status: PROPOSAL.** Nothing here is applied. The sheet still carries the original
> eight families. The apply checklist at the bottom is the entire change, expressed as
> Archetype-column values per treat id — apply any subset, in any order.
> Authored from: the archetype audit (all 90 sheet rows read), five full playtest runs
> (2026-08-01/02, including the first completed week), and the design owner's brief that
> archetypes remain a live design axis with mechanical roles ahead.

## What a family is (the rules this proposal follows)

1. **A family names a play pattern, not a return type.** "These treats multiply" is a
   phase; "these treats pay you for crowding cats together" is a strategy a player can
   build toward. Any family whose definition mentions add/mul/misc is misdesigned.
2. **One sentence, no "and also".** If the definition needs two clauses joined by "and
   also", it is two families (this is what killed Tribal *& Sculptors*).
3. **Input vs payout: the payout wins ties.** paid_leave *reads* discards but *pays*
   cash → it is money (Scrooge), not clockwork (Clutch). One rule, resolves every
   collision found in the audit.
4. **The voice is the office.** PF Inc. is a work-week fiction — Scrooge, Clutch,
   seniority, paid_leave, paycheck_advance. New names must sound like they were
   overheard in the same break room.

## Verdict on the current eight

| Current family | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Clutch** | **Keep name, replace definition** | The name is good — "the clutch moment" — but today it holds three unrelated clusters (single-cat boosts, discard treats, hand-clock treats). Redefined below as the resource-clock family, which the name genuinely describes. Its single-cat members leave for Spotlight. |
| **Flat Freight** | **RENAME → Manifest** | Criticized in the audit as the only family named after a return type — and the criticism stands, so the name goes. What its members actually share: each reads a **count** off the run (cards in deck, cats after this treat, rounds owned) and pays it flat. In shipping fiction, the document that lists what's in the box is the **manifest**. |
| **Gambler** | **Keep as-is** | Cleanest family in the sheet: dice and echoes. Loses only standing_ovation (a bag utility that happens to be random — the randomness is not the point of playing it). |
| **Huddle** | **Keep, tighten to "crowding"** | Defining it as *crowding* rather than *adjacency* makes bell its legitimate zero point (pays for pointedly NOT crowding) instead of an outlier. Loses lap_cat (single-target → Spotlight) and alley_gang (edge geometry → Lines & Terrain). |
| **Lines & Terrain** | **Keep as-is** | Coherent: the box itself — rows, columns, corners, edges, rubble, empty space. Gains alley_gang; loses zoomies (the only member that *writes* to the board instead of reading it). |
| **Purrfect Engine** | **Keep as-is** | Coherent: pays for filling the box. Loses only morning_stretch, which reads the hand clock and never looks at the fill — one move makes this family perfect. |
| **Scrooge** | **Keep as-is** | Coherent: money in, and the two treats (empty_bowl, fat_cat) that convert the wallet back into score — deliberately opposed, and the *reason* the family exists. Loses only bottomless_tote (a bag utility priced in dollars, not a money strategy). |
| **Tribal & Sculptors** | **RENAME → Tribal, evict the sculptors** | The "& Sculptors" half is two treats that never score a point. Without them the family is one sentence: pays for what your cats *are* — their type, their shape, their size. |

Why the five "keep" families survive a proposal that was invited to demolish them: the
audit's criticism was aimed at **definitions and membership**, not labels — Gambler,
Huddle, Lines & Terrain, Purrfect Engine and Scrooge each already reduce to one true
sentence once one or two misfiled members leave. Renaming a coherent family costs every
existing sheet row and player memory for zero design information. The two incoherent
names (Flat Freight, Tribal & Sculptors) both go.

## The proposed ten

### Clutch — *sized by the round's resource clocks: hands and discards.*
second_chance, fence_sitter, sprint_finish, frequent_flyer, nine_lives ←FF,
quick_paws ←FF, bench_warmer ←FF, poker_face ←FF, morning_stretch ←PE.
**Roadmap gap:** everything reads the clock; nothing *winds* it — pairs naturally with a
future Facilities "+1 discard this round" treat.

### Manifest (née Flat Freight) — *reads a count off the run and pays it flat.*
fish_flakes, toy_mouse, fashionably_late, deep_deck, seniority, big_bite,
crowd_pleaser ←Clutch; disabled: trophy_shelf, hot_streak.
**Roadmap gap:** all counts are upward-good; a risk variant ("+N per EMPTY bag cell")
would give the family a tension member.

### Spotlight — *everything aimed at one cat: make a star, then pay her.* **(new)**
red_carpet, head_scritches, opening_act, diva ←Clutch; lap_cat ←Huddle;
show_cat ←Tribal.
Play-proven across three runs (660/815/870-point pamper hands). Four of six members
already carry showbiz names; diva's own Explanation cell describes the build.
**Roadmap gaps:** the add ladder is one rung (red_carpet +80, then nothing to multiply —
a rare "+150 to the next cat" and an epic "+40 per cell it occupies" are the missing
rungs); every selector but two says "NEXT in scan" (LAST/SMALLEST/first-placed are free
design space); no downside capstone ("×4 the next cat, ×0.5 every other" is the
legendary this family wants).

### Facilities — *changes the run's furniture — deck, box, bag, rules, clock — and scores nothing itself.* **(new)**
jumping_ball, second_wind, soft_landing ←Clutch; zoomies ←L&T; brownies,
sardine_tin ←Tribal; standing_ovation ←Gambler; disabled: bottomless_tote ←Scrooge.
Named for the office fiction (the department that fixes the building). Extracting it
makes three other families' sentences true in the same stroke.
**Roadmap gaps — the sharpest in the whole system:** no common/rare rung (five of seven
are epic $7 — utility is unaffordable before Day 3); nothing refills the **discard
pool** (the most consequential missing treat since discards became a round resource);
nothing but the disabled tote touches the **bag** (the proven #1 constraint: five
blocked tier-1 purchases in the winning run alone); nothing removes a card from hand.

### Tribal (née Tribal & Sculptors) — *pays for what your cats are: their type, their shape, their size.*
tabby_pack, tuna_can, shadow_feast, silver_lining, purebred, one_shot, lone_kitty,
matching_set, gentle_giant, smol_bean; disabled: mittens, family_portrait,
hand_me_downs, top_cat, no_strays, milk_bar.
**Note:** four legendary rows differ only by colour (the ×2-type quartet) — candidates
for consolidation into one "PACK LEADER: ×2 each cat of a TYPE you choose at purchase"
if legendary-slot pressure ever matters.

### Huddle — *crowding: pays for what is (or pointedly isn't) packed beside it.*
milk, bell, snack_stack, cat_pile, potluck, litter_mates, frenzy, copycat, twin_paws.

### Lines & Terrain — *the box itself: rows, columns, corners, edges, rubble, empty space.*
catnip, feather, roadblock_party, window_seat, corner_office, personal_space,
rainbow_row, high_rise, full_house, alley_gang ←Huddle; disabled: demolition_crew.

### Purrfect Engine — *pays for filling the box.*
biscuit, gold_star, clean_plate, cuddle_puddle, string_theory, all_or_nothing,
purrfect_record.

### Scrooge — *money: mints cash, and spends the wallet back into score.*
gift_wrap, paycheck_advance, piggy_bank, lucky_penny, paid_leave, coin_purse,
empty_bowl, fat_cat.

### Gambler — *the dice and the echo: random payouts, and treats that retrigger or copy treats.*
laser, encore, wild_dice, loaded_dice, second_breakfast, treat_encore, catnado.

## Not proposed (examined and rejected)

- **A "scaling" family** — scaling is a property, not a play pattern; it would
  strip-mine four families to build one with no shared fiction. Fix: apply the existing
  `scaling` Strategy tag consistently to all 13 scalers (and delete the `expire` tag
  from lone_kitty/one_shot/purebred, none of which expire).
- **A discard family** — one Clutch move (poker_face) plus the input-vs-payout rule
  covers it.
- **A converter family** (empty_bowl/fat_cat) — two members, and they are Scrooge's
  reason to exist.
